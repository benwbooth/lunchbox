# Lunchpail user guide

Lunchpail brings your games, emulators, artwork, and controllers into one library.
You can use it at your desk or browse the same collection in Couch Mode.

New here? Start with [installation](installing.md), then
[add and play your first game](getting-started.md).

## Your library

- [Add and organize games](library.md) — import ROMs, match releases, search, and make collections.
- [Artwork, videos, and manuals](media.md) — browse media and connect optional providers.
- [Download games](downloads.md) — set up qBittorrent and review downloads.
- [Add your own download sources](custom-sources.md) — torrent files, magnets, and local catalogs.

## Playing

- [Emulators and launching games](playing.md) — choose an emulator, supply firmware, and fix launch problems.
- [Controllers](controllers.md) — choose players, record buttons, and save mappings.
- [Display shaders and bezels](display.md) — CRT and handheld LCD effects, artwork, fullscreen, and ultrawide screens.
- [Saves and backups](saves.md) — find your files, enable backups, and resolve conflicts.
- [Arcade games](arcade.md) — ROM sets, six-button layouts, and supported blood settings.

## Make it your own

- [Translations, mods, and cheats](patches.md) — find patches and apply them without changing your original game.
- [RetroAchievements](achievements.md) — sign in and choose Casual or Hardcore mode.
- [Live translation](translation.md) — set up local GPU translation for RetroArch.
- [Couch Mode](couch-mode.md) — wall, album, wheel, and shelf views.
- [Couch Mode themes](themes.md) — install a theme or make one.

## Help and maintenance

- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)
- [Move or back up your Lunchpail profile](profile-backups.md)
- [Build Lunchpail from source](building.md)

These guides describe the current source version. An older release may not
include every feature shown here; its [release notes](https://github.com/benwbooth/lunchpail/releases)
are the best place to check.

Games and BIOS files are not included. Use content you have the right to use.
